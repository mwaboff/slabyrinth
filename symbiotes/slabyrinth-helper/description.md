# Slabyrinth Helper

The TaleSpire side of Slabyrinth (slabyrinth.aboff.com), a desktop app where you design a map by chatting with Claude.

A big map does not fit in one slab, so Slabyrinth splits it into slabs that all share one bounding box. This add-on
puts them in your hand one at a time: raise the build plane (G), click one spot, press Next, click the same spot
again, until the map is complete. It always says what to do next.

- **Send from Slabyrinth:** with this panel open, send the map from Slabyrinth's build page.
- **Hotkeys:** while a map is being placed, Slabyrinth holds Ctrl+Alt+D (Next), Ctrl+Alt+A (Back) and Ctrl+Alt+S
  (Put in hand again), so your mouse can stay on the spot. Change them under Link and hotkeys. The buttons in this
  panel always work too.
- **Private:** it talks only to TaleSpire and to Slabyrinth on this computer (127.0.0.1), paired by a key that
  Slabyrinth writes when it installs the add-on.

Install and update it from the Slabyrinth app.
